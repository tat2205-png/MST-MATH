# v1.6 Controlled Pilot Package

## Participant instructions

1. Use a representative mathematics document; do not enter personal student information.
2. Complete PILOT-01 through PILOT-10 in order.
3. Record start/end time, retries, corrections, acceptance, and any F0–F4 issue.
