# v1.6 Classroom Validation Specification

Status: preparation only; classroom evidence is pending.

Evaluate student-facing worksheet/test exports and representative learning videos with a teacher present. Do not collect student identities or free-form student answers.

## Level-3 checks

- readability and mathematical clarity;
- worksheet usability and space for student work;
- figure relation, labels, coordinates, and overlap;
- video formula/figure readability, pacing, narration sync, and classroom display readability;
- student confusion points and teacher correction effort.

## Video field score

Score each dimension 0 (unusable), 1 (major correction), 2 (minor correction), or 3 (classroom ready). Mean score target is at least 2.5 for RC consideration. Do not mark this target passed before real evaluation.

## Stop conditions

F3/F4 issues stop the pilot output and block RC. In particular, mathematical corruption, source mutation, answer/solution leakage, privacy exposure, or unusable output is a critical failure.

## Evidence

Link each observation to a task ID and output hash or artifact identifier. Keep automated QA results, teacher feedback, and classroom observations in separate records.
