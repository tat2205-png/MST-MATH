# Visual System

## Master canvas design

Treat the full whiteboard or diagram area as a unified coordinate space. Every region (intro, analysis, derivation, graph, geometry, conclusion) occupies a stable spatial anchor.

## Color hierarchy

- Background: very light off-white or dark pedagogical slate.
- Primary strokes: high-contrast dark slate / bright cyan.
- Accents: emerald green (theorems/correct), amber (caution/traps), rose/crimson (key points/extrema).
- Hidden edges: dashed lines with 50% opacity.

## Typography and formulas

- Title text: clear, high legibility.
- Mathematical formulas: standard LaTeX with MathTex.
- Key results: highlight boxes with subtle background tint.

## Asset placement

- Place geometry figures and function graphs near their corresponding formula derivations.
- Ensure minimum 0.5 unit margins between adjacent regions to prevent visual clutter during camera pans.
