# STYLE_REFERENCE_FACEBOOK_V1

## Reference concept

A large infographic/mind-map acts as one continuous spatial world. The camera tells the story by moving between knowledge regions.

## Core formula

`ONE WORLD -> MANY KNOWLEDGE REGIONS -> CAMERA TELLS THE STORY`

Avoid rebuilding every shot as an unrelated slide when one coherent canvas can work.

## Visual language

- bright / very light neutral background;
- subtle low-contrast science/math doodles;
- colorful curved branch ribbons;
- soft shadows and restrained highlights;
- pastel rounded cards;
- strong focal formulas;
- SVG/transparent PNG supporting illustrations;
- consistent branch colors.

## Camera language

`OVERVIEW -> PUSH IN -> SETTLE -> READ -> MICRO PAN -> ZOOM OUT -> TRAVEL -> PUSH IN`

Use overview shots to maintain learner orientation.

## Reading rule

When important content is on screen, stabilize the camera long enough to read it.

## Ending

Prefer a final zoom-out to the master canvas or a meaningful high-level summary view.

## What not to imitate blindly

Do not sacrifice mathematical correctness for visual similarity. For math lessons, geometry, labels, plots, and formulas take precedence over reference-video decoration.
