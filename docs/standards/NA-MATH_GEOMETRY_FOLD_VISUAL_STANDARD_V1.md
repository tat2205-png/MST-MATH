The approved GeoGebra-like reference behavior is: a retained flat cut sheet at `t=0`, continuous rigid hinge rotation for `0<t<1`, and a closed target solid at `t=1`. GeoGebra is reference evidence only, not a source of truth or an adapter target for this task.
